VERITAS, dini vakitler ve kıble yönünün hassas hesaplanabilmesi amacıyla cihazınızın GPS (Konum) verilerine ihtiyaç duyar.

DESCRIPTION

VERITAS is an academic research and comparison platform designed for those who wish to examine sacred texts from linguistic and theological perspectives.

OUR COMMITMENT

By using this app, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and accepted these terms and our privacy policy.

01 Data Controller

Developed by Viorek Labs. User privacy is our core principle, and we use local storage architectures to ensure data security.

02 Data Processing & Location (GPS)

VERITAS requires location data to calculate sacred times and Qibla direction.

  • Real-time Access: Location is requested only while the app is in use. No background tracking.
  • Local Processing: Location data is processed locally on your device and never sent to external servers or shared with third parties.

03 Disclaimer

  • Translation Differences: Texts are compiled from globally recognized open-source databases. They may contain nuances different from the originals due to linguistic challenges.
  • Not a Religious Authority: VERITAS is an independent tech initiative and not affiliated with any religious institution. It is not a source of "fatwa" or legal decree.
  • Calculation Tolerances: The developer is not responsible for errors caused by GPS signal weakness or magnetic field deviations.

04 Academic Scope

All content provided is for theological research and academic comparison purposes. The user accepts these texts as academic data sources.

05 Ads & Analytics

Google AdMob is used for sustainability. Alcohol, gambling, crime, and adult content ads are manually blocked. Anonymous stats may be collected for performance monitoring.

06 Offline Work & Intellectual Property

VERITAS is designed to work offline. All design, "VERITAS" brand, and code rights belong to Viorek Labs.

07 Optional Cloud Backup

The app offers an optional Google Drive backup feature to protect your notes. This data is backed up to your personal Google account, not to Viorek Labs servers.

08 Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

09 Neutrality & Anti-Proselytism Statement

VERITAS does not support missionary activities, impose religious doctrines, or contain ideological orientation. The platform is designed solely for neutral information and providing scientific comparison tools for researchers.
